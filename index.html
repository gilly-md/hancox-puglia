Reservations to lock in

booking tips
✅ Booked
Grotte di Castellana · Thu 18 Jun

Booked — entrance 13:00, short "Partial Itinerary" tour (~50 min), English (order 454347; 3 tickets, toddler 0–5 free, €56 paid). Be at the entrance by 12:45 with the ticket PDF (not the email) — no ticket office needed. Caves are 14–18°C: warm layer + closed shoes, and carry the toddler (no stroller below).

✅ Booked
Ramunno Bistrot · Mon 15 Jun

Booked — 12:30, party of 3, Ostuni (TheFork conf. 1715812809). Seafood-led bistrot just off the main square (Via Giovanni Lanza 9), genuinely family-warm. Ask for the outdoor patio in shade; park below the old town and walk up (ZTL + stairs — baby carrier).

✅ Booked
Beach-day lido · Sun 14 Jun

Booked — Stella Maris (Torre Lapillo, also your lunch spot): umbrella + 2 loungers, rows 3–7 (front rows 1–2 were sold out, but these still get full umbrella shade for her nap — just a little further from the water). Paid ~€28; receipt in your private trip note. Be on the sand by ~09:00 to settle in; +€5 parking. Why this one: most-reviewed by far (1,700+), calmest, changing tables, and food brought to your lounger so you needn't move a settled child.
If plans change, backups that take non-guest bookings: Leucasia (lovely & well-kept, lounge-music vibe; Blue-Flag, changing cabins, guarded parking): WhatsApp +39 389 348 4913. Or Samanà (walk-in only — arrive early; guarded parking + lifeguard).

Pharmacy
Farmacia

Look for the green cross; typically open ~08:30–13:00 and 16:30–20:00. After hours, the door lists the nearest farmacia di turno (on-call). A guardia medica covers night/holiday calls.
